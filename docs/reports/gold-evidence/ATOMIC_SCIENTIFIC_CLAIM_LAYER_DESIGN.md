# Atomic Scientific Claim layer

An AtomicClaim is one adjudicable proposition with the qualifiers necessary to judge it. It records paper/document/experiment identity, claim type, structured subject-predicate-object, qualifiers, normalized and human-readable forms, criticality, attribution, evidence-bundle IDs, provenance, confidence, binding and verification state.

Observation, method, rationale, interpretation, association and mechanism remain distinct types. Atomicization must not delete conditions or controls, convert association to causation, turn rationale into observation, or mix experiments/papers. Composite legacy fields become review-required candidates—not asserted facts. Claim IDs derive from document identity, experiment identity, field/source-local identity and sorted locators.
