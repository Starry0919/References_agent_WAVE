# Lineage, Product, and Engineering Intelligence Report

Lineage decisions distinguish K12_EXACT, K12_DERIVATIVE_EXPLICIT, K12_DERIVATIVE_SUPPORTED, ECOLI_RELATED, ECOLI_NON_K12, ECOLI_UNRESOLVED, and NON_ECOLI. MG1655, W3110, and BW25113 are explicit derivatives; arbitrary E. coli strains are not assumed to be K-12.

Product decisions distinguish TARGET_PRODUCT, DIRECT/UPSTREAM_PRECURSOR, SUBSTRATE, BYPRODUCT, DERIVED_PRODUCT, RELATED_PATHWAY_PRODUCT, BACKGROUND_MENTION, and UNRESOLVED. 5-HTP, serotonin, indole, shikimate, and aromatic amino acids cannot silently become target-product evidence.

Engineering intelligence separates implemented genetic/metabolic/regulatory/transporter/enzyme/ALE/bioprocess actions from model-only, enzyme-only in vitro, mechanistic-only, and no-engineering records. Each decision reports source, confidence, evidence, and fulltext-verification state.
